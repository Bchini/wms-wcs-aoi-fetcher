Fetch raster data,
from one link.

Paste a service URL — a GetCapabilities link, a bare endpoint, or a full GetMap/GetCoverage request — and run it. The layer, area, CRS, and resolution are detected automatically; the clip itself runs entirely on your device.

Works with WMS or WCS. Give it a full GetMap/GetCoverage URL to clip exactly that BBOX, or just an endpoint to fetch the first layer's full extent.

Sized to this area — a small AOI can go much sharper than a full country extent.

Paste a link and press DETECT to see the layer and choose a resolution.
